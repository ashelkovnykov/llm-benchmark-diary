What credits bought

Combined quality gives the two rubrics equal weight after scaling: the average of app-as-a-percent and code-as-a-percent. Cost is on a log scale. Bubble area is passive time (how long the model ran). Grok models are not pictured, as they have no credit figures.

Quality per credit

Combined points divided by cost in credits. Higher score means more performance per credit. Keep this in mind, as it's possible for cheap broken output to score higher than expensive clean output.
